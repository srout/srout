# Saroj Rout, Ph.D.

Senior Member IEEE | Semiconductor Professional & Educator

---

### 🧬 About Me
I am a semiconductor professional, circuit designer, and academic mentor specializing in CMOS analog VLSI and open-source EDA ecosystems. My work bridges the gap between hardware innovation, open-source engineering, and next-generation technical education.

* 🎓 **Background:** Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering from [Tufts University](https://www.tufts.edu "Tufts University Website").
* 🔬 **Core Expertise:** Analog/Mixed-Signal IC Design, Power Management, Data Converters, and RISC-V Microcontrollers.
* 🚀 **Open Source:** Developing modern VLSI academic curricula utilizing open-source EDA tools.
* 🏢 **Industry & Academy:** Founder at Mixignal Innovations & Professor at [Silicon University](https://silicon.ac.in "Silicon University Official Site").

---

### 🛠️ Tech Stack & Tooling
* **Design & Simulation:** CMOS Analog/Mixed-Signal VLSI, RF Design, Data Converters, Metamaterials.
* **Open EDA & Hardware:** RISC-V, Open-source VLSI tools, Automated Test Environments.
* **Programming & Systems:** Python, C, Test Automation Frameworks.

---

### 📊 Research & Publications
* **Book:** Co-author of [*Active Metamaterials: Terahertz Modulators and Detectors*](https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-3-319-52219-7 "Active Metamaterials Springer Link") (Springer Nature).
* **Patents:** Co-inventor of 7+ patents in CMOS VLSI design and artificial electromagnetic materials.
* **Academic Committee:** Serving on the IEEE Solid-State Circuits Society Technical Committee on the Open Source Ecosystem (TC-OSE).
